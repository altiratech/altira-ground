# Altira Ground North Star

Status: long-term ambition document

## Product Ambition

Build the most useful project-first intelligence layer for real-asset transitions where value depends on change of use, infrastructure unlocks, entitlement progress, or hidden physical constraints.

## Why This Could Matter

- transition assets create high-value workflow fragmentation
- existing tools skew parcel-first, map-first, or static-data-first
- a strong dossier plus change-feed loop can expand into broader project and portfolio intelligence later

## Long-Term Product Shape

- future workflow layers:
  - compare mode
  - portfolio monitoring
  - richer entitlement intelligence
  - additional lenses such as ag-to-industrial or powered sites

## Expansion Logic

- natural expansion: broader transition-asset intelligence
- dangerous expansion: generic dashboard or parcel portal before the dossier loop is trusted
- what must remain true: project-first, provenance-first, workflow-first

## Suite / Platform Relationship

- standalone or suite module: standalone first, suite-compatible later
- what should be shared: identity direction, shared infra posture, design quality bar
- what should stay product-specific: ontology, workflow, and lens logic
